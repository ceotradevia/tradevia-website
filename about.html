Who We Are

About Tradevia

Tradevia is a consulting firm focused on delivering intelligent, impactful, and sustainable solutions for government, education, and healthcare sectors.

We combine domain expertise with the power of AI to help organizations innovate, transform and lead. Since our founding, we've partnered with public institutions and private organizations alike to modernize how they serve the people who depend on them.

Our team blends strategists, data scientists and engineers who understand that technology only matters when it improves real outcomes — for citizens, students and patients.
